### QUESTIONS
You are helping a writer capture their personal voice, so an AI can later rewrite their essay in a way that sounds like them.

Read the essay below and write 4 or 5 questions that ask the writer about their own real experience with this topic. The goal is to learn what they have seen, who they know, where they were, and what they think.

Rules for the questions:
- Ask about a real moment, place or person in their life connected to the topic: when they first came across it, who they talked to about it, what they noticed, what annoyed or amused them.
- Ask open questions that invite a short story, not yes/no questions or definitions.
- Do not suggest any language, slang, spelling, emoji or style for the answer. Never ask the writer to use pidgin, slang, typos or emoji. They answer however they normally speak or write.
- Do not ask for analysis, a summary, or an explanation of the topic.
- Keep each question to one plain sentence under 25 words.
- Do not turn the essay's own points into questions.

Return ONLY the questions, numbered 1 to 5, one per line. No intro, no extra text.
