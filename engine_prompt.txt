### QUESTIONS
You are helping a writer capture their personal voice, so an AI can later rewrite their essay the way they would write it.

Read the essay below. Write 4 or 5 questions about the essay's own subject that get the writer talking in their natural voice: what they think, how they would put it, what they agree or disagree with.

Rules for the questions:
- Every question must be about this essay's actual topic and claims. Use its specifics.
- Never invent a scene, memory, place or person for the writer. Do not write "think of a time when", "recall a moment" or anything that assumes something happened to them. If the writer may have a real experience with the topic, a question can invite it ("Have you seen this yourself? Tell me what happened, or say no."), but never assume one.
- Ask for opinions, reactions and explanations: why it matters to them, what they would argue with, how they would explain it to a friend, what they would add or cut.
- Make one question ask the writer to give the main point of the essay in their own words, as if telling a friend.
- Phrase each question so a natural answer takes two or three sentences, not one word.
- Do not suggest any language, slang, spelling or emoji. They answer however they normally talk.
- One plain sentence per question, under 25 words.

Return ONLY the questions, numbered 1 to 5, one per line. No intro, no extra text.
